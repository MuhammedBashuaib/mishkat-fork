Mishkat offline recitation for iOS

The bundled NVIDIA Arabic FastConformer CTC model and vocabulary are identical
to the Android assets. See NOTICE.txt for model provenance and CC-BY-4.0
attribution, SHERPA_ONNX_LICENSE.txt (Apache-2.0), and ONNX_RUNTIME_LICENSE.txt
(MIT). These files are distributed with the model in the application's asr/.

iOS inference libraries:
- Sherpa ONNX 1.13.8, official static iOS XCFramework
  https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/v1.13.8
  Archive SHA-256: 6b8e769cb153343270fdccbe92e3b3db0d1c421d67fa0989ab01fdf5b2fcf2de
- ONNX Runtime 1.28.2, the iOS dependency pinned by Sherpa ONNX 1.13.8
  https://github.com/csukuangfj/onnxruntime-libs/releases/tag/v1.28.2
  Archive SHA-256: 2c2299acbb461d26d4bac4bc85985d40e7c7177ed6072703ae0846d88b0b4599

Recognition runs locally on the CPU. No model download, account, or remote
speech service is required at runtime.
